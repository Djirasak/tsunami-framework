Card

Card

A white box with a 1px border. Each direct child (header, section, table, footer) is a part, separated by a line.

Usage

A header holds an h2 title, a p subtitle and an aside for buttons on the right. Content follows in section and footer.

Order #1042

Placed Oct 12, 2026 by Emma Wilson

3 items, $74.00 in total.

Updated 5 minutes ago

<article class="card">
  <header>
    <h2>Order #1042</h2>
    <p>Placed Oct 12, 2026 by Emma Wilson</p>
    <aside>
      <button type="button" class="btn" data-size="sm">Edit</button>
      <button type="button" class="btn" data-variant="primary" data-size="sm">Fulfill</button>
      <button type="button" class="btn" data-size="sm" popovertarget="card-more">
        More
        <i class="icon icon-chevron-down" aria-hidden="true"></i>
      </button>
      <div class="dropdown" id="card-more" popover>
        <button type="button" popovertarget="card-more" popovertargetaction="hide"><i class="icon icon-copy" aria-hidden="true"></i>Duplicate</button>
        <button type="button" popovertarget="card-more" popovertargetaction="hide"><i class="icon icon-printer" aria-hidden="true"></i>Print packing slip</button>
        <hr />
        <button type="button" data-variant="danger" popovertarget="card-more" popovertargetaction="hide"><i class="icon icon-trash-2" aria-hidden="true"></i>Delete</button>
      </div>
    </aside>
  </header>
  <section>
    <p>3 items, $74.00 in total.</p>
  </section>
  <footer>
    <p>Updated 5 minutes ago</p>
    <button type="button" class="btn">View all</button>
  </footer>
</article>

Header with a figure, its change and a link

Add a strong to the header and the h2 becomes a small gray label above the figure. In the aside, the link sits above the span[data-trend].

Needs attention: data-variant="warning"

A 2px warning-colored top border for a card that needs attention. danger, info and ok work the same way.

Payment disputed

Respond within 4 weeks

The cardholder claims the order was not received.

<section class="card" data-variant="warning" style="max-width: 28rem">
  <header>
    <h2>Payment disputed</h2>
    <aside><span class="badge" data-variant="warning">Respond within 4 weeks</span></aside>
  </header>
  <section>
    <p>The cardholder claims the order was not received.</p>
  </section>
</section>

Attributes

Attribute / element Values Description
.card article, section, div The card box. Every direct child gets padding and a divider above it
> header Card header: a two-column grid, text on the left, aside on the right
> header h2 / h3 Card title, 14px bold
> header p Grey subtitle under the title
> header > aside Buttons or badges on the right. Spans both rows when there is a title and a subtitle
> header > strong Large figure. Turns the h2 into a small gray label and stacks the aside vertically on the right
header [data-trend] up, down; empty = gray Change in the figure: up = ok color, down = danger color
> section, > div Content, with the same padding in every part
> footer Buttons on the right. The first p sits on the left in gray (such as a result count)
> .table, > .scroll-area > .table Edge-to-edge table. The first and last cells line up with the card padding
> .empty Empty state in place of a table
> .stat, > .stat-group Stat figures. See Stat
data-variant warning, danger, info, ok; omit for normal (default) 2px top border in the status color, for a card that needs attention
data-direction row; omit for stacked (default) Children sit side by side, separated by vertical lines. The first child is only as wide as its content (such as a date range picker next to a stat group)
