Page header

The top of a page: a breadcrumb on top, a back button, the page title and actions on the right.

Usage

An h1 title on the left, and the last div as a button group on the right. On narrow screens the buttons wrap to a new line.

Orders

Export New order 
<header class="page-header">
  <h1>Orders</h1>
  <div>
    <a href="#" class="btn">Export</a>

  </div>
</header>

Title block in a div

Attributes

Attribute / element Values Description
.page-header header Wrapping flex row, centered vertically
> nav nav.breadcrumb Breadcrumb on the top row, full width
> .btn Back button before the title. Does not grow or shrink (use data-size="icon" and aria-label)
> h1 Page title. Fills the space; a .badge inside follows the title
> hgroup An h1 title with a gray p under it
> div:first-child Older title block: breadcrumb, h1 and p stacked
> div:last-child Action buttons on the right (unless it is the first child or the title block after a nav)
p Gray secondary text
