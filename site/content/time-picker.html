Time picker

A time field made of separate segments for hour, minute and, if you want them, seconds and AM or PM. It has no popup: you type or use the arrow keys.

Hour and minute, with a starting time

Each part is its own segment. Type digits, or use the arrow keys. The form sends the time as HH:MM under data-name.

<div class="time-picker" data-name="start" value="09:30" aria-label="Start time"></div>

An empty field

Without a value the segments show -- and the form sends nothing. The first change fills the other segments with 00.

<div class="time-picker" data-name="opens" aria-label="Opening time"></div>

data-seconds adds seconds

The value becomes HH:MM:SS.

<div class="time-picker" data-name="logged" data-seconds value="14:05:09" aria-label="Logged at"></div>

data-hour12 shows 1 to 12 and AM or PM

Only what is shown changes: the value is still 24 hour, so 3:15 PM is sent as 15:15. Press A or P on the last segment.

<div class="time-picker" data-name="meeting" data-hour12 value="15:15" aria-label="Meeting time"></div>

data-min, data-max and data-step

Minutes move in steps of data-step. A time outside the limits is moved to the nearest one when focus leaves the field.

<div class="time-picker" data-name="slot" data-min="09:00" data-max="17:30" data-step="15" value="09:00" aria-label="Appointment time"></div>

Date and time in one input group

Put a Date picker and a time picker in one .input-group. The date field uses data-variant="ghost" so the group draws the only box.

<div class="input-group" style="max-width: 24rem">
  <span><i class="icon icon-calendar" aria-hidden="true"></i></span>
  <div class="date-picker" data-min="2026-01-01">
    <input type="text" class="input" data-variant="ghost" placeholder="YYYY-MM-DD" aria-label="Date" />
    <div data-popover aria-hidden="true"></div>
    <input type="hidden" name="date" />
  </div>
  <div class="time-picker" data-name="time" value="10:00" aria-label="Time"></div>
</div>

Sizes, invalid and disabled

data-size sets the height like an input. aria-invalid="true" draws the red border. disabled dims the field and turns the segments off.

<div class="stack" data-direction="row" data-gap="lg" style="flex-wrap: wrap">
  <div class="time-picker" data-size="sm" data-name="a" value="08:00" aria-label="Small"></div>
  <div class="time-picker" data-size="lg" data-name="b" value="08:00" aria-label="Large"></div>
  <div class="time-picker" data-name="c" value="25:00" aria-invalid="true" aria-label="Invalid"></div>
  <div class="time-picker" data-name="d" value="08:00" disabled aria-label="Disabled"></div>
</div>

Behavior

.time-picker is an empty element. The script fills it with the segments and a hidden input named by data-name, and keeps that input in the form's format: HH:MM, or HH:MM:SS with data-seconds. It is "" while a segment is empty. You can also write your own <input type="hidden"> inside; its name and value are used. A form reset puts the starting time back.

Arrow keys wrap inside a segment and never carry into the next one. Typed digits are limited to the segment's range, and a segment moves on once it cannot take another digit. Pasting HH:MM into any segment fills the whole field.

API

The script starts every .time-picker on the page, also one added later. On the element, value is read and set ("" or an invalid time clears it).

Methods

MethodDescription
window.tsunami.timePicker.get(el)Returns the value, HH:MM or HH:MM:SS, or "".
window.tsunami.timePicker.set(el, value)Sets the time and sends the events. An empty or invalid value clears it.
window.tsunami.timePicker.mount(el)Starts one element, for one made with a script before the page finished loading.
window.tsunami.timePicker.destroy(el)Removes the segments.

Keyboard

KeyDescription

←, →, :Move to the previous or next segment.
↑, ↓Add or take one, wrapping. Minutes follow data-step. On an empty segment, start from the lowest or the highest.
0–9Type the value. A segment moves on after two digits, or one that cannot take a second.

A, POn the AM or PM segment, set AM or PM.
Backspace, DeleteEmpty the segment.

Accessibility

The field is a role="group" named by its aria-label (default Time). Each segment is a role="spinbutton" with its own aria-label (Hour, Minute, Second, AM or PM), aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax, aria-valuenow and aria-valuetext (Empty while empty). Give the field an aria-label or a label that says what the time is for.
