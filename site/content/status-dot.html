Status dot

A small coloured dot with an optional label, for online state, health and sync status. It is quieter than a badge.

Statuses

Set data-status. Omit it for neutral.

Live

data-pulse adds a soft ring that repeats. It stops when the visitor prefers reduced motion, and the plain dot stays.

In a list

The dot sits on the middle of the line and follows the text colour. Without a label, give it a name for screen readers.

Attributes

Attribute / elementValuesDescription
.dotInline element: an 8px dot, then the label text if any.
data-statussuccess, warning, danger, info (omit for neutral)Dot colour, from the semantic colour variables.
data-pulsebooleanSoft repeating ring for a live state.

Accessibility

  • Colour alone is not enough. Keep a text label, or put role="img" and an aria-label on a dot without one.
  • The pulse is decoration and stops under prefers-reduced-motion.
