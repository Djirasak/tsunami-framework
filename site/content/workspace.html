Workspace

A full-window layout for one task that needs room. The top bar, side panels and work area scroll on their own.

Open full page

A workspace is the whole page

.workspace fills the window height (100dvh). Use it instead of the app shell (the app sidebar) for a single task, such as editing a theme. Its children are a <header> top bar (back button, <h1> task name, controls, and the primary button last), an <aside> side panel and a <main> work area.
